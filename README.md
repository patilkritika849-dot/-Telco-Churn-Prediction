### 🎯 Problem Statement
Telecom companies lose revenue when customers leave. This project analyzes 7043 customer records to predict who will churn, so the company can take action to retain them.

### 📈 Key Insights
1.  **Churn Rate:** 26.5% of customers left (1869 out of 7043).
2.  **Contract Impact:** Customers with Month-to-Month contracts have the highest churn rate.
3.  **Tenure Impact:** New customers (low tenure) are more likely to leave.

### 🤖 Model Performance
- **Model:** Random Forest Classifier
- **Accuracy:** 79.3%
- **Features Used:** Contract, Tenure, MonthlyCharges, InternetService etc.
